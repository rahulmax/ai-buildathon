Airtribe's 12-hour build sprint, Saturday 2026-10-24, 09:00 to 21:00 IST, Bengaluru.
Powered by Pine Labs. Registered as one team: Rahul and Chetan.

## What we're building

A voice invoice tool for freelancers and small sellers, in the **AI for Finance** track.

Say: "Invoice Acme ₹40,000 for landing page design, 18% GST, due in 7 days."
Get:

1. A well-designed GST invoice, at its own link.
2. A way to pay it (UPI, and Pine Labs if their test keys work).
3. A reminder message ready to send on WhatsApp.

Double-entry books sit underneath. They show as two views only: **who owes me** and
**GST this month**. The invoice gets people in; the books are the company.

Works in English, Hindi and Malayalam.

## Why this one

- Finance is the title sponsor's track, and the track text names "expense and invoice
  automation, small business accounting".
- Anyone in the room can use it with no sensitive data, which matters for the 20-user bar.
- A chats-to-invoices product (Chat2Cash) won Build India 2026 in Bengaluru. The category
  wins here, so ours has to look different: Indian-language voice, real books, a Pine Labs
  payment, and the design.

**Backup idea** if the finance build hits a wall early: a landing-page critic that scores
how generic a page looks ("sameness score"), in the Creative AI track.

## The bar

"Twenty real people, outside your team, using your product during the event."

The traction line we want on stage: **"₹X invoiced through this today"**, not just
"20 users".
