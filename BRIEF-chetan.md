The bar is 20 real people outside our team using the product during the event. Aim for 40
lined up, since half won't show.

**Tonight and before 09:00**

  developers, consultants, small shop owners. Name, phone, how you know them.
- Message each one: "I'm at a hackathon on Saturday. At 4pm I'll send you a link. It
  takes 1 minute: you say an invoice out loud and it makes one. Can you try it?"
- Keep a sheet: name, said yes, link sent, used it, invoice amount.

**On the day**
- 16:00: send the link to everyone who said yes. Personal message, not a group blast.
- 16:00 to 19:30: chase. Work the room too: builders here are freelancers too.
- Ask each user one question afterwards: "Would you use this for your real invoices?"
  Their quotes go on the traction slide.
- Watch `/stats` and tell Rahul the count every 30 minutes.
