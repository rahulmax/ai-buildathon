# Roadmap

What we show the judges: today's build is the start, and the books are where it goes.
Six months, starting the day after the event. Dates are targets, not promises.

**The idea in one line:** talk to make an invoice, snap to log a bill, and your books keep
themselves, in your own language.

**Who it's for first:** freelancers and one-person businesses in India: designers,
developers, consultants, tutors, small shops. People who send invoices and keep receipts,
and have no time or training for accounting software.

## Built today, 2026-10-24

- Say an invoice in Kannada, Hindi, Malayalam or English. Get a GST invoice with a link
- UPI QR on every invoice, Pine Labs payment link where keys allow
- WhatsApp reminder, one tap
- Snap a bill; it's logged with its GST
- Double-entry books underneath: who owes me, GST to pay this month

## Month 1, November 2026: make it something people keep using

- Sign in with a phone number (OTP), so your books follow you
- Your logo and colours on invoices
- Reminders that send themselves when an invoice is overdue
- Pine Labs live payments: the invoice marks itself paid
- Recurring invoices, for retainers
- Tamil, Telugu, Marathi and Bengali

## Month 2, December 2026: the books fill themselves

- Upload a bank statement; we match payments to invoices and bills for you
- Bills you haven't paid yet, and who you owe
- Profit in plain words: "You made ₹1,20,000 this month. Software was your biggest cost."
- Ask your books out loud: "How much has Ravi Traders paid me this year?"

## Month 3, January 2027: GST filing ready

- GSTR-1 ready from your invoices
- GSTR-3B summary from invoices and bills
- Input credit checked against what your sellers actually filed (GSTR-2B)
- Hand-off to your CA: an export, or invite them in

## Months 4 to 6, February to April 2027: the year end

The financial year ends on 31 March. That is the moment every freelancer thinks about their
books, and the deadline we build toward.

- Track the TDS your clients deducted, and match it to Form 26AS
- A year-end summary ready for your income tax return, including presumptive tax (44ADA)
  for professionals
- Bank data through the Account Aggregator network, via a licensed partner
- Working capital against unpaid invoices, with a lending partner

## How it makes money

To test, not settled:

- **Free:** invoices, bills, who owes me. Free is how people try it.
- **Paid, around ₹199 to ₹499 a month:** GST filing, CA hand-off, bank matching, year end.
- **Payments:** a share of the payment fee through Pine Labs.
- **Later:** lending against invoices, through a partner.

## Why us, not Vyapar, Khatabook or Zoho Books

They're forms you fill in. Ours is a sentence you say or a photo you take, in your own
language. Nobody types a ledger entry, and the books are right because the double entry is
underneath from day one, not added later.
